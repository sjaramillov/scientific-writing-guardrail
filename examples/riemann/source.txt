Riemann hypothesis: editorial teaching synopsis, 26 November 2026.
Audience: a graduate scientist reading about formal verification outside mathematics.
Question: what was formally established, and what is still being explained?

Every nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function has real part exactly 1/2. The zeros at negative even integers are called trivial.
On 21 November 2026, the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative certified the formal statement and proof of the classical Riemann hypothesis.
Rubinstein’s working guide reduces its central inequality to thirteen hypotheses and leaves those hypotheses unproved within the guide.
